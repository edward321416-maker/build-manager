# Problem Definition

**[DECISION]** Address the transition from a tenant's ambiguous housing report to an actionable maintenance task. Product and customer authority: [Project Charter](../PROJECT_CHARTER.md).

**[HYPOTHESIS]** Tenants may struggle to describe symptoms, managers may need repeated clarification, and resolution records may be difficult to retrieve for the same unit. These are questions to validate, not findings. Frequency, severity, economic impact, market size, and willingness to pay are **[TO VERIFY]**.

## Problem boundaries

Investigate intake information quality, triage uncertainty, handoffs, status visibility, and unit-level history. Distinguish inconvenience from urgent hazards and from legal disputes. Do not diagnose every fault or determine who is legally responsible.

## Validation plan

Test CLM-003 and CLM-004 using the landlord and tenant guides. Compare recent problematic and successful reports, current alternatives, and counterexamples. Record anonymous evidence and limitations in the [registries](../research/README.md). Do not import legacy market or competitor claims without checking original sources.

The problem statement can advance after concrete recent-event evidence supports a recurring unmet workflow need and competing explanations are documented. The evidence threshold and sample design are **[TO VERIFY]**, to be decided before calling the problem validated.
