# B2B2C Strategy

**[DECISION]** The customer target is landlords managing approximately 10–100 units and small/medium rental managers. Tenants in the properties are the primary reporting users. These roles define the intended service, not signed customers or proven buyers.

| Role | Proposed value | Validation gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Landlord / management firm | Receive structured tasks and review maintenance history | Adoption authority, workflow fit, and willingness to pay: TO VERIFY |
| Tenant | Explain a concern and understand status / next action | Usability, trust, accessibility, and channel preference: TO VERIFY |
| Human operator / service professional | Receive relevant facts for follow-up | Handoff requirements, availability, and responsibility boundaries: TO VERIFY |

**[HYPOTHESIS]** A manager-led introduction may support tenant adoption. Sales channels, onboarding ownership, tenant participation, vendor partnerships, and pricing are not selected or validated. No revenue, conversion, user count, or savings claim is made.

## Data and trust

The planned long-term asset combines unit, issue, treatment-result, and maintenance-history data. Maintain logical property/unit associations while limiting access to authorized parties. Public research identifiers must never map back to real homes. Exact tenancy access rules, retention periods, consent/legal basis, and deployment design require privacy review before a pilot.

## Learning sequence

Validate the problem and current alternatives, test a clearly labeled synthetic workflow, establish feasible safety and access boundaries, then recruit an authorized pilot and design neutral pricing research. See [MVP scope](mvp_scope.md) and [current workflow](current_user_workflow.md). All stages remain subject to evidence gates; repository creation is not product validation.
