- [ ] Every factual application sentence maps to a verified claim/source and exact locator.
- [ ] Decisions, hypotheses, assumptions, and missing information are visibly distinguished.
- [ ] No unsupported market size, price, willingness to pay, interview finding, competitor feature, user count, or quantified effect remains.
- [ ] Time periods, denominators, populations, and extrapolation limits match each source.
- [ ] Draft text and images make consistent claims; synthetic examples cannot appear as real customer evidence.
- [ ] Reviewer and review date recorded before final release.
