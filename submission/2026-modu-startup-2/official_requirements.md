# Official requirements — 2026 Modu Startup Project, second recruitment

Status: **PARTIALLY VERIFIED (2026-09-13)**. Primary authority is MSS Notice No. 2026-511 (`SRC-002`). The public call and attached notice resolve the program, eligibility, deadline, application mechanics, optional media, and first-stage evaluation. **Exact live application-form field labels, mandatory flags, and character limits remain [TO VERIFY] in the authenticated Modu Startup portal.** Keep Issue #1 open until that live-form check is recorded.

## 1. Authority and application channel

- Program: second recruitment for the 2026 `모두의 창업 프로젝트`.
- Primary organizer / authority: Ministry of SMEs and Startups; program operations differ by track.
- Official notice: MSS Notice No. 2026-511, published 2026-08-20 (`SRC-002`).
- Application channel: `www.modoo.or.kr`, online only.
- Applicant must complete platform membership / identity verification and final submission for the application to count as submitted.
- At initial application, the notice states there is **no separate mandatory submission file** beyond the online challenge application. Eligibility evidence is requested later from selected applicants.

## 2. Track decision for build-manager

### General / technology track

- Selection scale: 8,000 people at the first selection stage.
- Supported field: technology-startup fields broadly.
- Basic applicant type: pre-founder or founder within seven years, subject to the official exclusions and conditions.

### Local track

- Selection scale: 2,000 people.
- Intended for ideas creating value from local resources / local characteristics.

### Current project decision

`build-manager` is being prepared for the **general / technology track** because the proposed product is an AI-enabled rental-maintenance software/service rather than a place-specific local-resource business.

## 3. Eligibility — general / technology track

### Pre-founder

- Must have no business registration as of the notice date, including 2026-08-20, under the notice's definition and evidence rules.

### Existing founder

- Must be a representative whose business registration date is within seven years of the notice date.
- The public recruitment materials describe the eligible start-date window as 2019-08-21 through 2026-08-20.
- Existing founders are subject to the notice's different-industry (`이종창업`) requirement for later progression; the detailed determination uses the Korean Standard Industrial Classification rule stated in the notice.

### Exclusion / restriction check

Applicant-specific eligibility must be checked privately before submission. The official notice includes restrictions such as, among others:

- unresolved national/local tax delinquency outside listed exceptions;
- unresolved return/recoupment obligations outside listed exceptions;
- excluded startup sectors under the applicable law / notice;
- certain concurrent 2026 MSS startup-commercialization support participation;
- active participation sanctions in MSS startup-support programs;
- inability to open/use the required project account/card when applicable;
- specified personnel of the 2026 program's operating organizations;
- listed wage-arrears / subsidy-program exclusion conditions;
- specified prior first-cohort Modu Startup participation cases;
- other justified participation restrictions stated by MSS.

**Do not publish applicant tax, business-registration, financial, sanction, or identity documents in this public repository.** Record only an anonymized `eligible / review / ineligible` result after private checking.

## 4. Application deadline

- Application opens: **2026-08-20**.
- Hard close: **2026-09-17 at 16:00** (`CLM-011`).
- The notice states application submission closes at 16:00 and new applications are unavailable after closure.
- The public notice does not separately print an IANA/UTC timezone label. Operationally this is the Korean government program's local portal time; do not rewrite the official requirement as a formally stated timezone unless the portal itself confirms one.
- The notice recommends applying two to three days before the deadline because of possible congestion.

### Revision / receipt

- A submitted challenge application may be edited and resubmitted before the application deadline.
- After recruitment closes, submitted content cannot be modified or deleted under the notice rules.
- Final receipt should be verified through the platform after the submission-result notification.

## 5. Application materials

### Mandatory at initial application

- Online challenge application entered directly through the Modu Startup platform.
- Required identity / applicant declarations and consent presented by the portal.

### No separate mandatory file at initial application

- The call states that no separate submission document is required with the initial challenge application.
- Later-round eligibility verification documents are separately requested from selected applicants.

### Optional supporting media

- Idea-supporting images: **up to 10 images**.
- Accepted image formats stated in the notice: `jpg`, `png`, `gif`.
- Short-form introduction: approximately **30–60 seconds**, submitted as a URL.
- These are optional and should not replace the written application.

## 6. Mentor institution selection

- Applicant selects a mentor institution after reviewing each institution's incubation/selection plan and mentor pool.
- The first written screening uses mentor-institution-specific focus indicators in addition to the general evaluation direction.
- Therefore mentor-institution selection is a **submission-strategy decision**, not a cosmetic choice.
- Before final submission, capture the current published focus indicators for candidate institutions and compare them against `build-manager`.

## 7. General / technology-track first-stage evaluation

The attached official notice states that the initial idea screening is:

- **written evaluation**;
- conducted by **three responsible mentors** organized by the selected mentor institution;
- based on the challenge application;
- evaluates factors including **differentiation** and **effectiveness**;
- also follows the mentor institution's published focus indicators;
- selects the first-round entrants according to the announced scale and mentor-institution allocation reflecting application volume.

Implication for `build-manager`: the application must make the user problem, existing alternatives, AI-specific workflow advantage, expected effect, and execution feasibility understandable without an oral explanation.

## 8. Program progression — general / technology track

Current public structure:

1. Idea screening: applicants → 1R, 8,000 selected.
2. Regional preliminary stage: 8,000 → 2R, 1,000 selected.
3. Regional audition: 1,000 → 3R, 200 selected; public IR is introduced at later rounds.
4. National audition / Final: subsequent expert/public IR and final award selection.

The notice states that detailed schedules, scale, and process can change; later-round guidance controls when issued.
