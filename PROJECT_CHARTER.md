# Project Charter

Status: **[DECISION]** operator-approved scope, 2026-09-13. This document owns the product purpose and initial customer segment.

## Purpose

Turn a tenant's ambiguous housing concern into an actionable maintenance task for a landlord or manager, and retain the resolution history at unit level. The service accepts conversation and potentially photos/video; reliable media interpretation remains **[TO VERIFY]**.

## People and value

- Customer target: landlords managing about 10–100 units; small/medium rental-management firms.
- User target: tenants in studios, officetels, and multifamily rental housing.
- **[HYPOTHESIS]** Structured intake can reduce clarification burden and loss of maintenance history. Frequency, magnitude, adoption, and willingness to pay are unverified.
- Product principle: 세입자의 말 한마디를 집주인이 바로 처리할 수 있는 유지관리 업무로 변환한다.

## Initial scope

1. AI Guided Issue Intake.
2. AI Triage with a safety-first escalation boundary.
3. Landlord / Manager Dashboard.
4. Property / Unit Maintenance History.

Long-term asset: unit, issue, treatment-outcome, and maintenance-history data. Data collection must be necessary, permissioned, and access-controlled; accumulation is not permission to retain personal data indefinitely.

## Decision gates

M0 validates problem evidence. M1 prepares the Modu Startup 2 submission with an operator target of **2026-09-17**, pending official deadline verification. M2 defines testable MVP acceptance criteria. M3 prototypes with synthetic data. M4 pilots only after recruitment, consent, and safety readiness.

The operator owns product decisions. A future collaborator with Write access can merge without review approval. Collaborator identity is **PENDING**. No interview results, market size, price, traction, or performance numbers are established by this charter.
